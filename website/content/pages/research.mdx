# Research questions

AICA is at a documentation stage. The areas below are questions to be defined and evaluated; they are not completed research, test results, or validated capabilities.

## Propulsion assumptions

What lift, power, thermal, acoustic, and mechanical assumptions would need to be documented before a limited physical model could be responsibly specified?

## Stability and control

What response, disturbance, sensing, and correction requirements would define a bounded future control study? The use of automated or AI-assisted control remains a research direction, not a validated capability.

## Model instrumentation

Which force, vibration, temperature, power, and acoustic measurements would be necessary to compare model behavior with pre-recorded expectations?

## Materials and structure

What weight, stiffness, service access, fabrication, and durability constraints would apply to a small test article?

## Human-centered operation

How should future systems communicate state, operating boundaries, faults, and safe responses to operators and nearby people?

## Publication standard

Future research notes should identify status, method, operating conditions, limitations, and revision history. No result should be described as validated until suitable evidence is publicly available for review.
