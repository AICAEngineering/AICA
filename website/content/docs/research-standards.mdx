# Research standards

These standards define how AICA intends to document future engineering work. They are process commitments, not evidence that testing or validation has occurred.

## Traceability

Design decisions should connect to stated assumptions, calculations, observations, or test outcomes. Documents should carry a status, date, version, and limitations.

## Repeatability

Measurements should include configuration, method, equipment, inputs, environmental conditions, and known sources of uncertainty so the work can be understood and, where appropriate, repeated.

## Claim discipline

A single demonstration does not establish reliability. Public claims should remain narrower than or equal to the available evidence.

## Safety visibility

Known hazards, operating boundaries, anomalies, and unresolved failure modes should remain visible throughout development.

## Inconclusive results

Unsuccessful and inconclusive results should remain part of the record. They may lead to revision, delay, suspension, or a decision not to continue.
